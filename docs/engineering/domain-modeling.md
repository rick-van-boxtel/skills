## What it does

Clarify product terminology and record durable decisions. Reading a glossary alone does not require edits or an interview.

## When to reach for it

Type `/domain-modeling` when you want a requested terminology or domain-document change. It may also be selected when the requested task fits.

## Common questions

**Does every resolved term require an immediate edit?**

No. Capture authorized durable changes together when useful. Keep speculative ideas out of the glossary.

## It's working if

Product terms become clearer and only durable decisions enter documentation.

## Where it fits

Use it when design work changes the domain language, rather than for every code edit. For the available skills, see [ask-matt](https://aihero.dev/skills-ask-matt).
